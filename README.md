# 📺 Netflix Customer Churn Analysis | Power BI Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard that analyzes subscriber churn for a Netflix-style streaming service. It identifies which customer segments leave most often so that retention strategies can be targeted.

## 🎯 Business Problem
Subscriber churn directly reduces recurring revenue. This project answers:
- What percentage of customers are leaving?
- Which subscription plans have the highest churn?
- Does churn change with tenure, favorite genre, payment method or region?

## 📌 Key KPIs
| KPI | Value |
|---|---|
| Total Customers | 5K |
| Churned Customers | ~1.58K |
| Churn Rate | 31.66% |
| Retention Rate | 68.34% |
| Monthly Revenue | 20.31K |

## 🔍 Key Insights
- **Overall churn is about 32%**, so roughly 1 in 3 customers leaves.
- **Basic plan churns most (34.7%)**, followed by Standard (30.7%) and Premium (28.6%). Higher-tier customers are more loyal.
- **Standard is the largest segment** (~2K customers), so its churn has the biggest absolute impact.
- **Sci-Fi and Romance viewers show the highest churn**, while Documentary viewers show the lowest.
- **Debit card users churn slightly more** than PayPal and digital wallet users.
- **Churn by tenure fluctuates** between roughly 0.2 and 0.4, with no steady decline over time.
- Churn is compared across four regions: Asia-Pacific, Europe, Latin America and North America.

## 💡 Recommendations
1. Offer Basic users upgrade discounts or trial access to Premium features.
2. Run targeted content campaigns for Sci-Fi and Romance audiences.
3. Encourage customers to switch to PayPal or digital wallets with small incentives.
4. Launch retention offers for customers at high-risk tenure points.
